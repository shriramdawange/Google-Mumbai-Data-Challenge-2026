Data dictionary

The prediction target is hourly PM2.5 concentration in µg/m³. All target
intervals use IST (UTC+05:30). Train timestamps mark the end of the hour.

Train.csv
timestamp_ist: end of the hourly observation interval.
pm25: observed hourly PM2.5 concentration; the training target.
mean: the same concentration as pm25, retained from the source aggregation.
count: number of valid unique quarter-hour readings used; at least three.
available_at: retained source-file modification time for the original history;
for newly added API observations, the actual retrieval time of this snapshot.
This is not a verified first publication time or a guaranteed source delay.
location_id, sensor_id: OpenAQ station and PM2.5 sensor identifiers.
station_name: monitoring station name.
series_id: source-specific observation series identifier.
flag_high_1000: diagnostic flag for concentrations above 1,000 µg/m³;
these observations have not been removed automatically.

Test.csv
ID: unique station-hour identifier; its meaning and order remain fixed.
location_id, sensor_id: identifiers matching Train.csv and Stations.csv.
target_start_ist, target_end_ist: start and end of the one-hour target interval.
scoring_block: public, private or unscored allocation. Public allocation
records the original public allocation; only the active two-day
window contributes to the leaderboard at each update. It is not a
training feature describing future pollution.
unit: unit of the target concentration. No observed targets are included.

SampleSubmission.csv
ID: exactly the same 2,592 IDs and row order as Test.csv.
Target: fill every row with a finite, non-negative PM2.5 prediction.
The sample contains numeric example predictions (0.0). Replace them with your
model predictions; they are not observed targets or organizer answers.

Stations.csv
location_id: target station identifier.
station_name: station name.
selected_sensor: PM2.5 sensor_id used in Train.csv and Test.csv.
latitude, longitude: station coordinates in decimal degrees.
timezone: station time zone, Asia/Kolkata.

AdditionalHistory.zip
Optional Mumbai station history, with source identifiers preserved.
HistoryNotes.txt inside the archive explains the different schemas.
Historical source station IDs must not be assumed to match current
OpenAQ location IDs. No cross-source station mapping is supplied.

Evaluation periods
26 October launch: 22–23 October observations.
31 October update: 27–28 October observations.
5 November update: 3–4 November observations (revised to a fresher window).
9 November update: 5–6 November observations.
Each update replaces the previous public scoring window.
Private evaluation: 11–12 November observations only.
All dates are in 2026, using IST. The test grid also contains rows outside
these periods; these rows do not contribute to the announced scores.
The full 2,592-row submission remains required throughout.

Initial public: 22–23 October, active from launch on 26 October until the
31 October update. These observations are excluded from the launch Train.csv.
All 2,592 IDs remain required throughout the competition.

5 November 2026 release
Train contains 47,849 observed station-hours. Observations for earlier
public windows and 1–2 November are now available as historical training data.
Current public 3–4 November, next public 5–6 November, and final private
11–12 November remain excluded. Missing targets are not imputed.
Test.csv and SampleSubmission.csv remain byte-for-byte unchanged.
The launch scoring_block column is retained unchanged. For this update,
3–4 November IDs marked unscored at launch are selected by the organizer's
active public reference. It contains 92 valid observed targets out of 192.
